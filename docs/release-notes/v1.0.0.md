**Task manažér** is a personal, keyboard-first task manager (day / week /
month), with events, ideas, habits and a school timetable — a single-user web
app that installs on your phone and captures tasks offline. The UI is in
Slovak.

*Osobný task manažér na deň, týždeň a mesiac, s udalosťami, nápadmi,
návykmi a školským rozvrhom. Prvé verejné vydanie — návod na vlastné
nasadenie je v [NASADENIE.md](https://github.com/Richinino/task-manager/blob/v1.0.0/docs/NASADENIE.md).*

## Highlights

- **Two dates per task** — *planned for* vs. *due*, the core of the system —
  with a day plan that checks your time budget against meetings, school and
  events.
- **Quick capture in Slovak** — `zavolať Petrovi v piatok 15:00 !1 @telefon
  15m` becomes a scheduled, prioritised task.
- **Events and deadlines** — exams, deadlines, multi-day events, a suggested
  exam-prep plan, grades; included in export and search.
- **School timetable** from EduPage, with substitutions and holidays.
- **Reminders** via Web Push to phone and desktop; **Google Calendar**
  meetings (read-only).
- **Rituals, habits, ideas, learning, templates, `[[links]]`, archive** and a
  full JSON export.
- **PWA** — installable, offline capture queue.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Richinino/task-manager/blob/v1.0.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Self-hosting

Runs on free tiers of **Vercel + Neon + Google OAuth + cron-job.org**
(€0/month). Fork the repo and follow
[docs/DEPLOY.md](https://github.com/Richinino/task-manager/blob/v1.0.0/docs/DEPLOY.md)
(English) or
[docs/NASADENIE.md](https://github.com/Richinino/task-manager/blob/v1.0.0/docs/NASADENIE.md)
(Slovak, more detail). All environment variables are documented in
[`.env.example`](https://github.com/Richinino/task-manager/blob/v1.0.0/.env.example).
Database migrations run automatically on each production deploy.

**Requirements:** Node.js 24 (`.nvmrc`; with npm 10, `npm ci` rejects the
lockfile), Postgres (Neon) in production — locally an embedded PGlite needs
no setup.

## Known limitations

- **Slovak-only UI**, including the quick-capture syntax.
- **Single-user design.** Access is limited to an email allowlist
  (`ALLOWED_EMAILS`); several people can share a deployment with separate
  data, but it isn't a public service with sign-up. With an empty allowlist
  nobody can sign in in production.
- **Timetable sync is single-user** — one EduPage feed per deployment.
- **Offline** you can capture new tasks and view recently loaded screens;
  editing needs a connection.
- Google OAuth in *Testing* mode: calendar consent expires after 7 days.
- Developed and used on Android + desktop Chrome; iOS is untested.
